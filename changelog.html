• Music tracks in the printed script
    • Print Prep → Script → Music Tracks: each scene's track is printed as a small tag floating above its scene heading, and is clickable in a PDF. It takes no space on the page, so page breaks and formatting are exactly the same as without it.
  • Find & Replace
    • Ctrl+F opens a floating Find & Replace panel in the Write view (Ctrl+H, or ⌘⌥F on Mac, jumps straight to Replace). It doesn't block typing in the script.
    • Structure-aware: look in all text filtered by element type, or in just one part of your scene headings — INT/EXT, location, or time of day — with a list of the values your script already uses.
    • Character rename: cue lines keep their (V.O.) / (O.S.) / (CONT'D), mentions in action, dialogue and scene notes keep their case, and look-alikes (ANNABELLE, YOUNG ANNA) are never changed by accident.
    • Every match is listed with a checkbox and a preview of the change; Replace all is a single undo step.
  • Fixes
    • A scene's description and notes could be overwritten with another scene's after editing a scene number in Plan mode. Fixed.
    • Esc on the element-type menu no longer throws the cursor out of the script.
    • Portable build: Ctrl+E and the Ctrl+Enter element menu work again.
  • Change Case shortcut
    • Shift+F3 cycles selected text through UPPER → Sentence → Title → lower in Action, Dialogue and Parenthetical — across several lines, or on the word under the cursor. Remappable in Keyboard Shortcuts.
    • Sentence case: capitalises the first letter of each sentence and lowercases the rest — handy for turning ALL-CAPS text back into prose.
    • Shortcut recorder safety: browser shortcuts (Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+Tab…) are refused with an explanation, and while recording, an accidental tab-close asks “Leave site?” first.
  • Finish-line overhaul
    • Unified pagination engine: Page View, print, reports and FDX export now agree on page counts and scene lengths; long speeches and paragraphs split correctly across pages with proper (MORE)/(CONT'D) cues; scene headings never strand at a page bottom.
    • Keyboard fixes: element cycling moved to Ctrl+E (customizable, validated against conflicts); Ctrl+S now saves; Ctrl+Shift shortcuts no longer hijack text selection; undo/redo restores your cursor.
    • Modals unified: Escape and backdrop-click close every dialog; focus is trapped and restored for keyboard users.
    • Multi-tab safety: opening SFSS in a second tab no longer risks silently overwriting your work.
    • Fountain authoring: forces (. ! @ >), centered text, [[notes]], **bold**, *italic*, _underline_; imports preserve sections, synopses and boneyard content.
    • A4 paper support, hardened offline mode, and collaboration session robustness.
